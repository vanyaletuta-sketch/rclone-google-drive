Privacy Policy

This application is a private OAuth configuration used only by the repository owner to connect rclone to their personal Google Drive account.

The application does not operate a public service and does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information with third parties.

Access to Google Drive data is used only for synchronization performed by rclone on the owner's own computer.

OAuth credentials and access tokens are stored locally by rclone on the owner's computer and are not intentionally transmitted to this website.

Google Drive data is processed only as required to perform file synchronization requested by the account owner.
